# BCBC 2024 — Division A, Part 1 Compliance
## Section 1.1. General

> Source: British Columbia Building Code 2024, Division A, Part 1 Compliance, Section 1.1
> Pages: 1-2 to 1-10

---

## 1.1.1. Application of this Code

### 1.1.1.1. Application of this Code

**Sentence (1)** — This Code applies to any one or more of:
- a) design and construction of a **new building**
- b) **occupancy** of any building
- c) **change in occupancy** of any building
- d) **alteration** of any building
- e) **addition** to any building
- f) **demolition** of any building
- g) **reconstruction** of a building damaged by fire, earthquake, or other cause
- h) correction of an **unsafe condition** in or about any building
- i) all parts affected by a change in occupancy
- j) work necessary to ensure safety in parts:
  - i) remaining after demolition
  - ii) affected by but not directly involved in alterations
  - iii) affected by but not directly involved in additions
- k) installation, replacement, or alteration of regulated materials/equipment (except as permitted by BC Fire Code)
- l) work to ensure safety in a **relocated building** during and after relocation
- m) **safety during construction**, including protection of the public
- n) design, installation, extension, alteration, renewal or repair of **plumbing systems**
- o) alteration, rehabilitation and change of occupancy of **heritage buildings**

**Sentence (2)** — This Code does **NOT** apply to:
- a) sewage, water, electrical, telephone, rail, or similar public infrastructure in streets or public transit ROW
- b) utility towers, poles, communication aerials/towers (except loads on buildings)
- c) mechanical/other equipment not specifically regulated
- d) flood control and hydro electric dams
- e) accessory buildings **< 10 m²** that do not create a hazard
- f) temporary buildings (with AHJ permission): construction offices, seasonal storage, special events, emergency facilities
- g) factory-built housing complying with CSA-Z240 MH Series (exemption excludes on-site prep, services, appliances)
- h) areas specifically exempted by provincial or federal enactments

**Sentence (3)** — Applies to both **site-built** and **factory-constructed** buildings.

**Sentence (4)** — Farm buildings shall conform to the **National Farm Building Code of Canada 1995**.

**Sentence (5)** — For **heritage buildings**, Table 1.1.1.1.(5) alternate compliance methods may substitute for Division B requirements.

**Sentence (6)** — For **alterations to add a secondary suite** (not new additions/buildings), Table 1.1.1.1.(6) alternate compliance methods may substitute for Division B requirements.

### 1.1.1.2. Application to Existing Buildings

**Sentence (1)** — Where a building is altered, rehabilitated, renovated, or repaired, or there is a change in occupancy, the **level of life safety and building performance shall not be decreased** below the level that already exists.

---

## 1.1.2. Internal References to this Code

### 1.1.2.1. Book I and Book II

**Sentence (1)** — The BCBC consists of two books:
- **Book I** — General
- **Book II** — Plumbing Systems

### 1.1.2.2. Internal References

**Sentence (1)** — Unless a Book is specified, references to "the British Columbia Building Code," "the Code," or "this Code" shall be read as references to the Book in which they appear.
