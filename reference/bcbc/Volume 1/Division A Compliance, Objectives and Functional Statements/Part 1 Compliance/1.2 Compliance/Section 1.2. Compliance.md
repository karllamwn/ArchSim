# BCBC 2024 — Division A, Part 1 Compliance
## Section 1.2. Compliance

---

## 1.2.1. Compliance with this Code

### 1.2.1.1. Compliance with this Code

**Two paths to compliance:**

| Path | Method | Authority |
|------|---------|-----------|
| a) **Acceptable Solution** | Follow Division B provisions directly | No AHJ approval needed |
| b) **Alternative Solution** | Different approach that meets the same performance level as Division B | Must be accepted by AHJ under Division C Section 2.3 |

**Sentence (2)** — For alternative solutions under Clause (1)(b): the objectives and functional statements used to evaluate compliance shall be those in **Subsection 1.1.2. of Division B**.

**Sentence (3)** — An alternative solution **cannot** replace an acceptable solution if the acceptable solution requires conformance to a **provincial enactment** other than Book I or Book II of the BCBC.

---

### 1.2.1.2. Responsibility of Owner

**Sentence (1)** — Unless otherwise specified, the **owner** is responsible for carrying out the provisions of this Code.

**Sentence (2)** — The owner is **NOT relieved** of responsibility by:
- a) the AHJ granting a building permit
- b) the AHJ approving drawings or specifications
- c) the AHJ carrying out inspections

---

## 1.2.2. Materials, Appliances, Systems and Equipment

### 1.2.2.1. Characteristics

**Sentence (1)** — All materials, appliances, systems and equipment shall have the necessary characteristics to **perform their intended functions** when installed.

### 1.2.2.2. Storage on the Building Site

**Sentence (1)** — All materials, appliances and equipment on site shall be **stored to prevent deterioration** or impairment of essential properties.

### 1.2.2.3. Used Materials, Appliances and Equipment

**Sentence (1)** — Used materials/appliances/equipment are **permitted to be reused** when they:
- Meet the requirements of this Code **for new materials**, AND
- Are **satisfactory for the intended use**

---

## 1.2.3. Installation of Plumbing Systems

### 1.2.3.1. Personnel Performing Plumbing Work

**Sentence (1)** — Personnel performing installation, alteration, renewal or repair of a **plumbing system** shall:
- a) possess a **Canadian tradesperson's qualification certification as a plumber**, OR
- b) be an **indentured apprentice** supervised by a qualified journeyperson, OR
- c) be the **registered owner and occupant** (or intended occupant) of the **single detached dwelling** where the work occurs
