# BCBC 2024 — Division A, Part 1 Compliance
## Section 1.3. Divisions A, B and C of this Code

---

## 1.3.1. General — Scope of Each Division

| Division | Contains | Parts |
|----------|----------|-------|
| **Division A** | Compliance/application provisions, **objectives** and **functional statements** | Parts 1, 2, 3 |
| **Division B** | **Acceptable solutions** (the actual technical rules) | Parts 1–10 |
| **Division C** | **Administrative provisions** | Parts 1, 2 |

### 1.3.1.4. Internal Cross-references
Where the Division of a referenced provision is not specified, it is assumed to be in the **same Division** as the referencing provision.

---

## 1.3.2. Application of Division A

### 1.3.2.1.
**Parts 1, 2 and 3** of Division A apply to **all buildings** covered by this Code.

---

## 1.3.3. Application of Division B

### 1.3.3.1. Parts 1, 7, 8, and 10 — Universal Application
**Parts 1, 7, 8, and 10** of Division B apply to **all buildings**.

### 1.3.3.2. Parts 3, 4, 5 and 6 — Part 3 Buildings

**Parts 3, 4, 5 and 6** apply to buildings that are:

| Condition | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| a) **Post-disaster buildings** | Hospitals, emergency centres, power stations, etc. |
| b) **Group A, B, or F1** | Assembly / Care-Treatment-Detention / High-hazard industrial — **regardless of size** |
| c) **Group C, D, E, or F Div 2&3** AND exceeding thresholds | **> 600 m²** building area **OR** **> 3 storeys** in building height |

> **Key threshold:** Group C/D/E/F2/F3 buildings **≤ 600 m² AND ≤ 3 storeys** → use **Part 9** instead.

### 1.3.3.3. Part 9 — Small Buildings

**Part 9** applies to buildings that are **ALL** of:
- ≤ **3 storeys** in building height, AND
- ≤ **600 m²** in building area, AND
- Used for: Group C (residential), Group D (business/personal services), Group E (mercantile), or Group F Div 2&3

> Note: Group A, B, and F1 are **never** Part 9 — they always use Part 3.

### Quick Decision Table

| Occupancy Group | Size | Applicable Part |
|----------------|------|-----------------|
| A (Assembly) | Any | Part 3 |
| B (Care/Treatment/Detention) | Any | Part 3 |
| F1 (High-hazard industrial) | Any | Part 3 |
| Post-disaster | Any | Part 3 |
| C, D, E, F2, F3 | > 600 m² OR > 3 storeys | Part 3 |
| C, D, E, F2, F3 | ≤ 600 m² AND ≤ 3 storeys | **Part 9** |

---

## 1.3.3.4. Building Size Determination

**Sentence (1)** — Where a **firewall** divides a building, each portion is considered a **separate building** (unless specifically modified elsewhere in the Code).

**Sentence (2)** — Portions separated by a **vertical fire separation ≥ 1 h** (continuous through all storeys and service spaces) may be treated as separate buildings for **building height** purposes, provided:
- a) Each portion is **≤ 4 storeys** and used only for **assembly, residential, or business and personal services** occupancies, AND
- b) Unobstructed firefighter path from nearest street to each entrance ≤ **45 m**

**Sentence (3)** — The vertical fire separation in (2) may terminate at the floor immediately above a **basement**, provided the basement conforms to **Article 3.2.1.2. of Division B**.

> See Note A-1.3.3.4.(2) re: sloping sites — stepped buildings on slopes may appear taller than they are; each stepped portion uses its own grade level to determine building height.

---

## 1.3.4. Application of Division C

### 1.3.4.1.
**Parts 1 and 2** of Division C apply to **all buildings**.
