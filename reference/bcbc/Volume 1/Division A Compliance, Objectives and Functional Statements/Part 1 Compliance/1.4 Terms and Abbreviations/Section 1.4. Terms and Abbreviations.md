# BCBC 2024 — Division A, Part 1 Compliance
## Section 1.4. Terms and Abbreviations

---

## 1.4.1. Definitions of Words and Phrases

### 1.4.1.1. Non-defined Terms

**Sentence (1)** — Words not listed in Article 1.4.1.2. shall have **commonly assigned meanings** in context,
taking into account specialized trade/professional usage.

**Sentence (2)** — "Objectives and functional statements" → see Parts 2 and 3.

**Sentence (3)** — "Acceptable solutions" → provisions in Parts 2–9 of Division B.

**Sentence (4)** — "Alternative solutions" → Clause 1.2.1.1.(1)(b).

---

### 1.4.1.2. Defined Terms

> Words and terms appearing in **italics** in the BCBC have the following meanings.
> Organized alphabetically — use Ctrl+F to search.

---

#### A

**Access / Accessible** — An area and its facilities that is easy to approach, enter, exit, operate,
participate in, pass to and from, and use safely and independently by persons with disabilities.

**Access to exit** — That part of a *means of egress* within a *floor area* that provides access to an *exit*
serving the floor area.

**Adaptable dwelling unit** — A *dwelling unit* designed with some *accessible* features and which
accommodates future modification to provide more accessible features.

**Adfreezing** — Adhesion of *soil* to a *foundation unit* resulting from freezing of soil water.
Also referred to as "frost grip."

**Air barrier system** — The assembly installed to provide a continuous barrier to the movement of air.

**Air-supported structure** — A structure consisting of a pliable membrane that achieves and maintains
its shape and support by internal air pressure.

**Alarm signal** — An audible signal transmitted throughout a zone or zones or throughout a *building*
to advise occupants that a fire emergency exists.

**Alert signal** — An audible signal to advise designated persons of a fire emergency.

**Alteration** — A change or extension to any matter or thing or to any *occupancy* regulated by this Code.

**Apparent sound transmission class (ASTC)** — A single number rating of the airborne sound attenuation
of *building* assemblies separating two adjoining spaces, taking into account both the direct and flanking
sound transmission paths.

**Appliance** — A device to convert fuel into energy; includes all components, controls, wiring and piping
required to be part of the device by the applicable standard referred to in this Code.

**Artesian groundwater** — A confined body of water under pressure in the ground.

**Assembly occupancy (Group A)** — The *occupancy* or use of a *building* or part thereof by a gathering
of persons for civic, political, travel, religious, social, educational, recreational or like purposes,
or for the consumption of food or drink.

**Attic or roof space** — The space between the roof and the ceiling of the top *storey* or between a
dwarf wall and a sloping roof.

**Authority having jurisdiction (AHJ)** — The governmental body responsible for the enforcement of any
part of this Code or the official or agency designated by that body to exercise such a function.

---

#### B

**Basement** — A *storey* or *storeys* of a *building* located below the *first storey*.

**Bearing surface** — The contact surface between a *foundation unit* and the *soil* or *rock* upon which
it bears.

**Boiler** — An *appliance* intended to supply hot water or steam for space heating, processing or power
purposes.

**Braced wall band** — An imaginary continuous straight band extending vertically and horizontally through
the *building* or part of the *building*, within which *braced wall panels* are constructed.

**Braced wall panel** — A portion of a wood-frame wall where bracing, sheathing, cladding or interior
finish is designed and installed to provide the required resistance to lateral loads due to wind or
earthquake.

**Breeching** — A *flue pipe* or chamber for receiving *flue* gases from one or more *flue* connections
and for discharging these gases through a single *flue* connection.

**Building** — Any structure used or intended for supporting or sheltering any use or *occupancy*.

**Building area** — The greatest horizontal area of a *building* above *grade* within the outside surface
of exterior walls or within the outside surface of exterior walls and the centre line of *firewalls*.

**Building height (in storeys)** — The number of *storeys* contained between the roof and the floor of
the *first storey*.

**Business and personal services occupancy (Group D)** — The *occupancy* or use of a *building* or part
thereof for the transaction of business or the rendering or receiving of professional or personal services.

---

#### C

**Caisson** — See *Pile*.

**Care** — The provision of services other than *treatment* by or through care facility management to
residents who require these services because of cognitive, physical or behavioural limitations.

**Care occupancy (Group B, Division 3)** — The *occupancy* or use of a *building* or part thereof where
*care* is provided to residents.

**Cavity wall** — A construction of masonry units laid with a cavity between the wythes. The wythes are
tied together with metal ties or bonding units, and are relied on to act together in resisting lateral loads.

**Chimney** — A primarily vertical shaft enclosing at least one *flue* for conducting *flue* gases to the
outdoors.

**Chimney liner** — A conduit containing a *chimney flue* used as a lining of a *masonry or concrete
chimney*.

**Closure** — A device or assembly for closing an opening through a *fire separation* or an exterior wall
(e.g., door, shutter, damper, wired glass, glass block); includes all hardware, closing devices, frames
and anchors.

**Combustible** — A material that **fails** to meet the acceptance criteria of CAN/ULC-S114,
"Standard Method of Test for Determination of Non-Combustibility in Building Materials."

**Combustible construction** — That type of construction that does not meet the requirements for
*noncombustible construction* or *encapsulated mass timber construction*.

**Combustible dusts** — Dusts and particles that are ignitable and liable to produce an explosion.

**Combustible fibres** — Finely divided, combustible vegetable or animal fibres presenting a flash fire
hazard in a loose, unbaled condition (cotton, wool, hemp, sisal, jute, kapok, paper, cloth).

**Combustible liquid** — A liquid having a *flash point* at or above **37.8°C** and below **93.3°C**.

**Conditioned space** — Any space within a *building* whose temperature is controlled by provision of
heating or cooling over substantial portions of the year.

**Constructor** — A person who contracts with an *owner* to undertake a project; includes an *owner*
who contracts with more than one person for work on a project.

**Contained use area** — A supervised area containing one or more rooms in which occupant movement is
restricted to a single room by security measures not under the control of the occupant.

**Cooktop** — A cooking surface having one or more burners or heating elements.

**Coordinating registered professional** — A *registered professional* retained under Clause 2.2.7.2.(1)(a)
of Division C to coordinate all design work and field reviews of the *registered professionals* required
for a project.

---

#### D

**Dangerous goods** — Products, materials or substances regulated by TC SOR/2001-286 (TDGR) or
classified as controlled products under HC SOR/2015-17 (Hazardous Products Regulations / WHMIS).

**Dead load** — The weight of all permanent structural and non-structural components of a *building*.

**Deep foundation** — A *foundation unit* that provides support by transferring loads to *soil* or *rock*
at considerable depth by end-bearing, adhesion or friction. Piles are the most common type.

**Designer** — The person responsible for the design.

**Detention occupancy (Group B, Division 1)** — The *occupancy* by persons who are restrained from or
are incapable of evacuating to a safe location without assistance because of security measures not under
their control.

**Direct-vented** — An *appliance* and its venting system in which all combustion air is supplied directly
from outdoors and products of combustion are vented directly to outdoors via independent, totally enclosed
passageways connected directly to the *appliance*.

**Distilled beverage alcohol** — A beverage produced by fermentation containing more than **20% by volume**
of water-miscible alcohol.

**Distillery** — A *process plant* where *distilled beverage alcohols* are produced, concentrated or
otherwise processed.

**Dwelling unit** — A *suite* operated as a housekeeping unit, used or intended to be used by one or more
persons and usually containing cooking, eating, living, sleeping and sanitary facilities.

---

#### E

**Encapsulated mass timber construction** — That type of construction in which fire safety is attained by
use of encapsulated mass timber elements with an *encapsulation rating* and minimum dimensions for
structural members and other *building* assemblies.

**Encapsulation rating** — The time in minutes that a material or assembly will delay the ignition and
combustion of encapsulated mass timber elements when exposed to fire under specified conditions of test.

**Excavation** — The space created by the removal of *soil*, *rock* or *fill* for the purposes of
construction.

**Exhaust duct** — A duct through which air is conveyed from a room or space to the outdoors.

**Exit** — That part of a *means of egress*, including doorways, that leads from the *floor area* it
serves to a separate *building*, an open public thoroughfare, or an exterior open space protected from
fire exposure from the *building* and having access to an open public thoroughfare.

**Exit level** — The level of an *exit* stairway at which an exterior *exit* door or *exit* passageway
leads to the exterior.

**Exit storey** — A *storey* having an exterior *exit* door.

**Exposing building face** — That part of the exterior wall of a *building* that faces one direction and
is located between ground level and the ceiling of its top *storey* or, where a *building* is divided into
*fire compartments*, the exterior wall of a *fire compartment* that faces one direction.

---

#### F

**Factory-built chimney** — A *chimney* consisting entirely of factory-made parts designed to be assembled
without requiring fabrication on site.

**Farm building** — A *building* or part thereof that does not contain a *residential occupancy* and is
associated with farming; used essentially for housing equipment or livestock, or production/storage/
processing of agricultural and horticultural produce or feeds.

**Field review** — A review of work at a building site (and where applicable at fabrication locations) that
a *registered professional* in their professional discretion considers necessary to ascertain whether the
work substantially complies in all material respects with the plans prepared by a *registered professional*.

**Fill** — *Soil*, *rock*, rubble, industrial waste or organic material transported and placed on the
natural surface; may or may not be compacted.

**Fire block** — A material, component or system that restricts the spread of fire within a concealed
space or from a concealed space to an adjacent space.

**Fire compartment** — An enclosed space in a *building* separated from all other parts by enclosing
construction providing a *fire separation* having a required *fire-resistance rating*.

**Fire damper** — A *closure* consisting of a damper installed in an air distribution system or wall/floor
assembly; normally held open but closes automatically in the event of a fire to maintain the integrity of
the *fire separation*.

**Fire detector** — A device that detects a fire condition and initiates an electrical signal to actuate
an *alert signal* or *alarm signal*; includes *heat detectors* and *smoke detectors*.

**Fire load** — The *combustible* contents of a room or *floor area* expressed as average weight of
*combustible* materials per unit area; includes furnishings, finished floor, wall and ceiling finishes,
trim and temporary/movable *partitions*.

**Fire-protection rating** — The time in minutes or hours that a *closure* will withstand the passage of
flame when exposed to fire under specified conditions of test.

**Fire-resistance rating (FRR)** — The time in minutes or hours that a material or assembly of materials
will withstand the passage of flame and the transmission of heat when exposed to fire under specified
conditions of test.

**Fire-retardant-treated wood** — Wood or a wood product with surface-burning characteristics (flame
spread, rate of fuel contribution, density of smoke) reduced by impregnation with fire-retardant chemicals.

**Fire separation** — A construction assembly that acts as a barrier against the spread of fire.

**Firestop** — A system consisting of material, component and means of support used to fill gaps between
*fire separations* or around items that wholly or partially penetrate a *fire separation*.

**Fire stop flap** — A device for use in horizontal assemblies required to have a *fire-resistance rating*;
operates to close off a duct opening through the protective ceiling membrane in event of a fire.

**Firewall** — A type of *fire separation* of *noncombustible construction* that subdivides a *building*
or separates adjoining *buildings* to resist the spread of fire; has a prescribed *fire-resistance rating*
and structural stability to remain intact under fire conditions for the required fire-rated time.

**First storey** — The uppermost *storey* having its floor level **not more than 2 m above grade**.

**Flame-spread rating** — An index or classification indicating the extent of spread-of-flame on the
surface of a material as determined in a standard fire test.

**Flammable liquid** — A liquid having a *flash point* **below 37.8°C** and having a vapour pressure not
more than 275.8 kPa (absolute) at 37.8°C.

**Flash point** — The minimum temperature at which a liquid gives off vapour in sufficient concentration
to form an ignitable mixture with air near the surface of the liquid.

**Flight** — A series of steps between landings.

**Floor area** — The space on any *storey* of a *building* between exterior walls and required *firewalls*,
including the space occupied by interior walls and *partitions*, but **not** including *exits*, *vertical
service spaces*, and their enclosing assemblies.

**Flue** — An enclosed passageway for conveying *flue* gases.

**Flue collar** — The portion of a fuel-fired *appliance* designed for attachment of the *flue pipe* or
*breeching*.

**Flue pipe** — The pipe connecting the *flue collar* of an *appliance* to a *chimney*.

**Forced-air furnace** — A *furnace* equipped with a fan that provides the primary means for circulation
of air.

**Foundation** — A system or arrangement of *foundation units* through which the loads from a *building*
are transferred to supporting *soil* or *rock*.

**Foundation unit** — One of the structural members of the *foundation* of a *building* (footing, raft
or pile).

**Frost action** — The phenomenon when water in *soil* is subjected to freezing, resulting in a total
volume increase or build-up of expansive forces, and subsequent thawing that leads to loss of soil
strength.

**Furnace** — A space-heating *appliance* using warm air as the heating medium; usually has provision for
attachment of ducts.

---

#### G

**Gas vent** — That portion of a venting system designed to convey vent gases to the outdoors from the
*vent connector* of a gas-fired *appliance*.

**Grade** — The lowest of the average levels of finished ground adjoining each exterior wall of a
*building*, except that localized depressions need not be considered.

**Greenhouse agricultural occupancy (Group G, Division 3)** — An agricultural *occupancy* where plants
are grown in a *building* primarily constructed of roofs and walls designed to transmit natural light.

**Groundwater** — A free standing body of water in the ground.

**Groundwater level** — The top surface of a free standing body of water in the ground (also:
groundwater table).

**Guard** — A protective barrier around openings in floors or at the open sides of stairs, landings,
balconies, *mezzanines*, galleries, raised *walkways* or other locations to prevent accidental falls
from one level to another.

---

#### H

**Heat detector** — A *fire detector* designed to operate at a predetermined temperature or rate of
temperature rise.

**Heavy timber construction** — That type of *combustible construction* in which fire safety is attained
by placing limitations on the sizes of wood structural members and on the thickness and composition of
wood floors and roofs and by the avoidance of concealed spaces.

**Heritage building** — A *building* which is legally protected or officially recognized as a heritage
property by the Provincial government or a local government.

**High-hazard agricultural occupancy (Group G, Division 1)** — An agricultural *occupancy* containing
sufficient quantities of highly *combustible* and flammable or explosive materials which constitute a
special fire hazard.

**High-hazard industrial occupancy (Group F, Division 1)** — An *industrial occupancy* containing
sufficient quantities of highly *combustible* and flammable or explosive materials which constitute a
special fire hazard.

**Horizontal exit** — An *exit* from one *building* to another by means of a doorway, vestibule,
*walkway*, bridge or balcony.

**Horizontal service space** — A space (attic, duct, ceiling, roof or crawl space) oriented essentially
horizontally, concealed and generally inaccessible, through which building service facilities such as
pipes, ducts and wiring may pass.

---

#### I

**Impeded egress zone** — A supervised area in which occupants have free movement but require the
release, by security personnel, of security doors at the boundary before they can leave; does not include
a *contained use area*.

**Indirect service water heater** — A *service water heater* that derives its heat from a heating medium
such as warm air, steam or hot water.

**Industrial occupancy (Group F)** — The *occupancy* or use of a *building* or part thereof for the
assembling, fabricating, manufacturing, processing, repairing or storing of goods and materials.

**Interconnected floor space** — Superimposed *floor areas* or parts of *floor areas* in which *floor*
assemblies that are required to be *fire separations* are penetrated by openings without *closures*.

---

#### L

**Limiting distance** — The distance from an *exposing building face* to a property line, the centre line
of a *street*, lane or public thoroughfare, or to an imaginary line between 2 *buildings* or *fire
compartments* on the same property, measured at right angles to the *exposing building face*.

**Live load** — A variable load due to the intended use and *occupancy* to be assumed in the design of
the structural members of a *building*; includes loads due to cranes and the pressure of liquids in
containers.

**Loadbearing** — Subjected to or designed to carry loads in addition to its own *dead load*, excepting
a wall element subjected only to wind or earthquake loads.

**Low-hazard industrial occupancy (Group F, Division 3)** — An *industrial occupancy* in which the
*combustible* content is **not more than 50 kg/m² or 1,200 MJ/m²** of *floor area*.

---

#### M

**Major occupancy** — The principal *occupancy* for which a *building* or part thereof is used or
intended to be used; includes subsidiary *occupancies* that are an integral part of the principal
*occupancy*.

| Classification | Description |
|---|---|
| A1 | Assembly — production and viewing of the performing arts |
| A2 | Assembly — not elsewhere classified in Group A |
| A3 | Assembly — arena type |
| A4 | Assembly — occupants gathered in the open air |
| B1 | Detention occupancies |
| B2 | Treatment occupancies |
| B3 | Care occupancies |
| C | Residential occupancies |
| D | Business and personal services occupancies |
| E | Mercantile occupancies |
| F1 | High-hazard industrial occupancies |
| F2 | Medium-hazard industrial occupancies |
| F3 | Low-hazard industrial occupancies |

**Masonry or concrete chimney** — A *chimney* of brick, stone, concrete or masonry units constructed on
site.

**Means of egress** — A continuous path of travel for escape of persons from any point in a *building*
to a separate *building*, an open public thoroughfare, or an exterior open space with access to a public
thoroughfare; includes *exits* and *access to exits*.

**Mechanically vented** — An *appliance* and its combustion venting system in which products of
combustion are entirely exhausted to outdoors by a mechanical device (fan, blower or aspirator); sealed
downstream of the fan (no draft hood or draft control device).

**Medium-hazard industrial occupancy (Group F, Division 2)** — An *industrial occupancy* in which the
*combustible* content is **more than 50 kg/m² or 1,200 MJ/m²** and not classified as *high-hazard*.

**Mercantile occupancy (Group E)** — The *occupancy* or use of a *building* or part thereof for the
displaying or selling of retail goods, wares or merchandise.

**Mezzanine** — An intermediate floor assembly between the floor and ceiling of any room or *storey*;
includes an interior balcony.

---

#### N

**Noncombustible** — A material that **meets** the acceptance criteria of CAN/ULC-S114 for
Non-Combustibility in Building Materials.

**Noncombustible construction** — That type of construction in which fire safety is attained by use of
*noncombustible* materials for structural members and other *building* assemblies.

---

#### O

**Occupancy** — The use or intended use of a *building* or part thereof for the shelter or support of
persons, animals or property.

**Occupant load** — The number of persons for which a *building* or part thereof is designed.

**Open-air storey** — A *storey* in which at least **25%** of the total area of its perimeter walls is
open to the outdoors to provide cross-ventilation.

**Owner** — Any person, firm or corporation controlling the property under consideration during the
period of application of Sentence 1.1.1.1.(1) of this Code.

---

#### P

**Partition** — An interior wall 1 *storey* or part-*storey* in height that is not *loadbearing*.

**Party wall** — A wall jointly owned and jointly used by 2 parties under easement agreement or by right
in law, erected at or upon a line separating 2 parcels of land each of which is, or is capable of being,
a separate real-estate entity.

**Perched groundwater** — A free standing body of water in the ground extending to a limited depth.

**Persons with disabilities** — Persons who have a permanent or temporary physical, mental, intellectual
or sensory impairment which, in interaction with various barriers, may hinder their full and effective
participation in society.

**Pile** — A slender *deep foundation unit* made of wood, steel or concrete (or a combination), either
premanufactured and placed by driving/jacking/screwing, or cast-in-place (often referred to as caissons).

**Plenum** — A chamber forming part of an air duct system.

**Plumbing system** — A drainage system, a venting system and a water system or parts thereof.

**Post-disaster building** — A *building* determined by the *authority having jurisdiction* as necessary
for the provision of essential services to the general public in the event of a disaster; includes:
hospitals, emergency treatment facilities, telephone exchanges, power generating stations, electrical
substations, control centres for natural gas distribution, water treatment/storage facilities, sewage
facilities, emergency response facilities, fire/rescue/police stations, communications facilities.

**Private sewage disposal system** — A privately owned plant for the treatment and disposal of sewage
(e.g., septic tank with absorption field).

**Process plant** — An *industrial occupancy* where materials, including *flammable liquids*,
*combustible liquids* or gases, are produced or used in a process.

**Protected floor space** — That part of a *floor area* protected from the effects of fire and used as
part of a *means of egress* from an *interconnected floor space*.

**Public corridor** — A corridor that provides *access to exit* from more than one *suite*.

**Public way** — A sidewalk, *street*, highway, square or other open space to which the public has
access as of right or by invitation.

---

#### R

**Ramp** — A path of travel having a slope **steeper than 1 in 20**.

**Registered professional** — A person registered as an Architect with the AIBC, or as a professional
engineer or professional licensee engineering with APEGBC, under the *Professional Governance Act*.

**Registered professional of record** — A *registered professional* retained to undertake design work
and *field review* in accordance with Subsection 2.2.7. of Division C.

**Repair garage** — A *building* or part thereof where facilities are provided for the repair or
servicing of motor vehicles.

**Residential occupancy (Group C)** — The *occupancy* or use of a *building* or part thereof by persons
for whom sleeping accommodation is provided but who are not harboured for the purpose of receiving *care*
or *treatment* and are not involuntarily detained.

**Return duct** — A duct for conveying air from a space being heated/ventilated/air-conditioned back to
the HVAC *appliance*.

**Rim joist** — The outermost member in floor framing (other than blocking), be it parallel,
perpendicular or on an angle to the floor joists.

**Rock** — That portion of the earth's crust that is consolidated, coherent and relatively hard; a
naturally formed, solidly bonded mass of mineral matter that cannot readily be broken by hand.

**Run** — The horizontal distance between two adjacent tread nosings on a stair.

---

#### S

**Sanitary drainage system** — A drainage system that conducts sewage.

**Secondary suite** — A self-contained *dwelling unit* located within a *building* or portion of a
*building* that is:
- completely separated from other parts of the *building* by a vertical *fire separation* ≥ 1 h,
extending from ground or lowermost assembly continuously through or adjacent to all *storeys* and spaces;
- in a *building* of only *residential occupancy* containing only one other *dwelling unit* and common
spaces; and
- where both *dwelling units* constitute a single real estate entity.

**Service room** — A room provided in a *building* to contain equipment associated with building
services.

**Service space** — Space provided in a *building* to facilitate or conceal the installation of building
service facilities such as chutes, ducts, pipes, shafts or wires.

**Service water heater** — A device for heating water for plumbing services.

**Shallow foundation** — A *foundation unit* that derives its support from *soil* or *rock* located
close to the lowest part of the *building* it supports.

**Smoke alarm** — A combined *smoke detector* and audible alarm device designed to sound an alarm
within the room or *suite* in which it is located upon the detection of smoke.

**Smoke detector** — A *fire detector* designed to operate when the concentration of airborne combustion
products exceeds a predetermined level.

**Soil** — The fragmentary portion of the earth's crust; includes boulders, cobbles, gravel, sand, silt,
clay and organic matter.

**Solid masonry** — Single wythe or multi-wythe construction made of *solid masonry units* or
semi-solid, cored, or hollow masonry units (cells may or may not be filled with mortar or grout).

**Solid masonry unit** — A concrete block, clay brick, or calcium silicate brick unit whose **net solid
area is at least 75%** of its gross area.

**Sound transmission class (STC)** — A single number rating of the airborne sound attenuation of a
*building* assembly separating two adjoining spaces, taking into account the direct sound transmission
path only.

**Space heater** — A space-heating *appliance* for heating the room or space within which it is located,
without the use of ducts.

**Space-heating appliance** — An *appliance* intended for supplying heat to a room or space directly
(space heater, fireplace, unit heater) or through a heating system (central furnace or boiler).

**Sprinklered** — The *building* or part thereof is equipped with a system of automatic sprinklers.

**Stage** — A space designed primarily for theatrical performances with provision for quick-change
scenery and overhead lighting; traditionally separated from the audience by a proscenium wall and
curtain opening.

**Storage garage** — A *building* or part intended for the storage or parking of motor vehicles with no
provision for repair or servicing.

**Storage-type service water heater** — A *service water heater* with an integral hot water storage tank.

**Storey** — That portion of a *building* situated between the top of any floor and the top of the floor
next above it; if no floor above, between the top of such floor and the ceiling above it.

**Stove** — An *appliance* intended for cooking and space heating.

**Street** — Any highway, road, boulevard, square or improved thoroughfare **≥ 9 m in width**, that has
been dedicated or deeded for public use and is accessible to fire department vehicles and equipment.

**Subsurface investigation** — The appraisal of subsurface conditions at a building site by geological
surveys, in situ testing, sampling, visual inspection, laboratory testing, and groundwater observations.

**Suite** — A single room or series of rooms of complementary use, operated under a single tenancy;
includes *dwelling units*, individual guest rooms in motels/hotels/boarding houses, individual stores and
individual or complementary rooms for *business and personal services occupancies*.

**Supply duct** — A duct for conveying air from HVAC *appliance* to a space to be heated, ventilated or
air-conditioned.

---

#### T

**Tapered tread** — A tread with non-parallel edges that increases or decreases its *run* uniformly over
its width.

**Theatre** — A place of public assembly for production/viewing of performing arts or screening/viewing
of motion pictures; consists of auditorium with permanently fixed seats.

**Treatment** — The provision of medical or other health-related intervention to persons, where the
administration or lack thereof may render them incapable of evacuating to a safe location without
assistance.

**Treatment occupancy (Group B, Division 2)** — The *occupancy* or use of a *building* or part thereof
for the provision of *treatment*, and where overnight accommodation is available to facilitate the
treatment.

---

#### U

**Unit heater** — A suspended space heater with an integral air-circulating fan.

**Unprotected opening** — A doorway, window or opening not equipped with a *closure* having the required
*fire-protection rating*; or any part of a wall forming part of the *exposing building face* with
*fire-resistance rating* less than required for the *exposing building face*.

**Unsafe condition** — Any condition that could cause undue hazard to the life, limb or health of any
person authorized or expected to be on or about the premises.

**Unstable liquid** — A liquid (including *flammable liquids* and *combustible liquids*) that is
chemically reactive to the extent that it will vigorously react or decompose at or near normal
temperature and pressure, or is chemically unstable when subjected to impact.

---

#### V

**Vapour barrier** — The elements installed to control the diffusion of water vapour.

**Vent connector** — The part of a venting system that conducts the *flue* gases or vent gases from the
*flue collar* of a gas *appliance* to the *chimney* or *gas vent*; may include a draft control device.

**Vertical service space** — A shaft oriented essentially vertically that facilitates installation of
building services including mechanical, electrical and plumbing installations and elevators, refuse
chutes and linen chutes.

---

#### W

**Walkway** — A covered or roofed pedestrian thoroughfare used to connect 2 or more *buildings*.
