# BCBC 2024 — Division A, Part 1 Compliance
## Section 1.5. Referenced Documents and Organizations

---

## 1.5.1. Referenced Documents

### 1.5.1.1. Application of Referenced Documents

**Sentence (1)** — Provisions of documents referenced in this Code apply **only to the extent** they relate to:
- a) **buildings**, and
- b) the **objectives and functional statements** attributed to the applicable acceptable solutions in Division B

> Referenced documents do NOT apply in full — only the portions relevant to buildings and the linked objectives apply. (See Note A-1.5.1.1.(1))

**Sentence (2)** — Where a provision references the **BC Fire Code**, the **NECB**, or **Book II (Plumbing Systems)**, the applicable objectives and functional statements shall **include those found in the referenced document**.

### 1.5.1.2. Conflicting Requirements

**Sentence (1)** — In case of conflict between this Code and a referenced document, the **provisions of this Code govern**.

### 1.5.1.3. Applicable Editions

**Sentence (1)** — Referenced documents shall be the **editions designated in Subsection 1.3.1. of Division B**.

> To find which edition of a standard applies, check Division B Subsection 1.3.1.

---

## 1.5.2. Organizations

### 1.5.2.1. Abbreviations of Proper Names

**Sentence (1)** — Abbreviations of proper names (e.g., CSA, ULC, NFPA, ASTM) shall have the meanings assigned in **Article 1.3.2.1. of Division B**.

> For a full list of organization abbreviations (CSA, ULC, NFPA, ASTM, CAN, etc.), see Division B Article 1.3.2.1.
