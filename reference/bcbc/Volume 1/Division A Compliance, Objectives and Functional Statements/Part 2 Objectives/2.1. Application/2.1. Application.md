# BCBC 2024 — Division A, Part 2 Objectives
## Section 2.1 — Application of Part 2

---

### 2.1.1. Application of Part 2

**2.1.1.1.(1)** Part 2 applies to all buildings governed by this Code.

---

### 2.1.1.2. Limitations on Application of Specific Objectives

The following limitations restrict which objectives apply to particular building types:

**(1)** Objective **OS4** (Resistance to Unwanted Entry) applies only to buildings that contain a **dwelling unit**.

**(2)** Objective **OH3** (Noise Protection) applies only to buildings that contain a **dwelling unit**.

**(3)** Objective **OH5** (Hazardous Substances) applies only in respect of requirements in **Book II** of the BC Building Code and the **BC Fire Code**.

**(4)** Objective **OA** (Accessibility) does not apply to:
- Buildings of **Group F, Division 1** occupancy, or
- Buildings that are not occupied on a **daily or regular basis** by their users

**(5)** Objective **OA** does not apply to the portions of buildings exempted from accessibility requirements under Part 3 or Part 9 of Division B.

**(6)** Objective **OE** (Environment) applies only in respect of buildings governed by **Section 9.36.** of Division B.

> Section 9.36. of Division B covers energy efficiency requirements for buildings governed by Part 9 (small buildings). See Note A-2.1.1.2.(6) in Notes to Part 2.

---

### Summary Table — Objectives with Restricted Application

| Objective | Restriction |
|-----------|-------------|
| OS4 — Resistance to Unwanted Entry | Buildings containing dwelling units only |
| OH3 — Noise Protection | Buildings containing dwelling units only |
| OH5 — Hazardous Substances | Via Book II / BC Fire Code only |
| OA — Accessibility | Not Group F Div 1; not non-daily-occupied buildings |
| OE — Environment | Part 9 buildings governed by Section 9.36. only |

All other objectives (OS1, OS2, OS3, OS5, OH1, OH2, OH4, OP1–OP4, OE1, OE2) apply to **all buildings** governed by this Code.
