# BCBC 2024 — Division A, Part 2 Objectives
## Section 2.2 — Objectives

> **2.2.1.1.(1)** The objectives of this Code are as set out in this Section.

---

## OS — Safety

**OS** — To limit the probability that, as a result of the design or construction of the building, a person in or adjacent to the building will be exposed to an unacceptable risk of injury.

---

### OS1 — Fire Safety

**OS1** — To limit the probability that, as a result of the design or construction of the building, a person in or adjacent to the building will be exposed to an unacceptable risk of injury due to fire.

**OS1.1** — To limit the probability that fire will start as a result of the design or construction of the building.

**OS1.2** — To limit the probability that, if fire starts in a building, it will spread beyond the room or suite of origin.

**OS1.3** — To limit the probability that, if fire starts in a building, a person in the building will be unaware of the fire.

**OS1.4** — To limit the probability that, if fire starts in a building, a person in or adjacent to the building will be exposed to uncontrolled fire or smoke.

**OS1.5** — To limit the probability that, if fire starts in a building, a person's ability to safely evacuate from the building will be impaired.

---

### OS2 — Structural Safety

**OS2** — To limit the probability that, as a result of the design or construction of the building, a person in or adjacent to the building will be exposed to an unacceptable risk of injury due to structural failure.

**OS2.1** — To limit the probability that the building will be subject to structural failure as a result of dead loads and live loads.

**OS2.2** — To limit the probability that the building will be subject to structural failure as a result of wind loads.

**OS2.3** — To limit the probability that the building will be subject to structural failure as a result of earthquake loads.

**OS2.4** — To limit the probability that the building will be subject to structural failure as a result of snow and rain loads.

**OS2.5** — To limit the probability that the building will be subject to structural failure as a result of temperature change.

**OS2.6** — To limit the probability that the building will be subject to structural failure as a result of soil, hydrostatic or dynamic loads.

---

### OS3 — Safety in Use

**OS3** — To limit the probability that, as a result of the design or construction of the building, a person in or adjacent to the building will be exposed to an unacceptable risk of injury while using the building.

**OS3.1** — To limit the probability that a person will fall due to the design or construction of the building.

**OS3.2** — To limit the probability that a person will be injured due to the design or construction of stairs, ramps, escalators, elevators, dumbwaiters, material lifts, or other mechanical means of vertical transportation.

**OS3.3** — To limit the probability that a person will be injured due to contact with doors, windows, or other moveable elements.

**OS3.4** — To limit the probability that a person will be injured due to contact with or proximity to a sharp, hot, or high-voltage element in or on the building.

**OS3.5** — To limit the probability that a person will be injured due to an explosion caused by or in the building.

**OS3.6** — To limit the probability that a person will be injured due to exposure to substances hazardous to health.

**OS3.7** — To limit the probability that a person will be injured due to the design or construction of parking facilities.

---

### OS4 — Resistance to Unwanted Entry

*(Applies only to buildings containing dwelling units — see Section 2.1.1.2.)*

**OS4** — To limit the probability that a person in a dwelling unit will be exposed to an unacceptable risk of injury due to unwanted entry into the dwelling unit.

**OS4.1** — To limit the probability that an unauthorized person will gain entry into a dwelling unit through doors.

**OS4.2** — To limit the probability that an unauthorized person will gain entry into a dwelling unit through windows.

---

### OS5 — Construction and Demolition Site Safety

**OS5** — To limit the probability that, as a result of construction or demolition activities, a person in or adjacent to the site will be exposed to an unacceptable risk of injury.

**OS5.1** — To limit the probability that a person will be injured due to falling objects from a construction or demolition site.

**OS5.2** — To limit the probability that a person will be injured due to collapse of an excavation.

**OS5.3** — To limit the probability that a person will be injured due to contact with an unshored or undermined structure.

**OS5.4** — To limit the probability that a person will be injured due to contact with machinery on a construction or demolition site.

**OS5.5** — To limit the probability that a person will be injured on a construction or demolition site due to fire.

**OS5.6** — To limit the probability that a person will be injured due to contact with temporary structures or equipment on a construction or demolition site.

**OS5.7** — To limit the probability that a person will be injured due to the condition of temporary structures or equipment used in construction.

**OS5.8** — To limit the probability that a person will be injured due to the use of explosives in construction or demolition.

**OS5.9** — To limit the probability that a person will be injured due to inadequate safety measures on a construction or demolition site.

---

## OH — Health

**OH** — To limit the probability that, as a result of the design or construction of the building, a person in or adjacent to the building will be exposed to an unacceptable risk to health.

---

### OH1 — Indoor Conditions

**OH1** — To limit the probability that, as a result of the design or construction of the building, a person in the building will be exposed to unacceptable indoor conditions.

**OH1.1** — To limit the probability that a person in the building will be exposed to thermal conditions unacceptable for habitation.

**OH1.2** — To limit the probability that a person in the building will be exposed to lighting conditions unacceptable for habitation.

**OH1.3** — To limit the probability that a person in the building will be exposed to air quality conditions unacceptable for habitation.

---

### OH2 — Sanitation

**OH2** — To limit the probability that, as a result of the design or construction of the building, a person in or adjacent to the building will be exposed to unsanitary conditions.

**OH2.1** — To limit the probability that potable water will be contaminated as a result of the design or construction of the building's water supply system.

**OH2.2** — To limit the probability that a person in the building will be exposed to unsanitary conditions due to inadequate sanitary facilities.

**OH2.3** — To limit the probability that a person in or adjacent to the building will be exposed to unsanitary conditions due to inadequate drainage or sewage systems.

**OH2.4** — To limit the probability that a person in or adjacent to the building will be exposed to unsanitary conditions due to inadequate solid waste management.

**OH2.5** — To limit the probability that a person in or adjacent to the building will be exposed to unsanitary conditions due to vermin, insects, or other organisms.

---

### OH3 — Noise Protection

*(Applies only to buildings containing dwelling units — see Section 2.1.1.2.)*

**OH3** — To limit the probability that, as a result of the design or construction of the building, a person in a dwelling unit will be exposed to unacceptable levels of noise.

**OH3.1** — To limit the probability that a person in a dwelling unit will be exposed to unacceptable levels of noise from other parts of the building or from outside the building.

---

### OH4 — Vibration

**OH4** — To limit the probability that, as a result of the design or construction of the building, a person in the building will be exposed to unacceptable levels of vibration.

**OH4.1** — To limit the probability that a person in the building will be exposed to vibration transmitted through the building structure.

---

### OH5 — Hazardous Substances

*(Applies via Book II / BC Fire Code only — see Section 2.1.1.2.)*

**OH5** — To limit the probability that, as a result of the design or construction of the building, a person in or adjacent to the building will be exposed to hazardous substances.

**OH5.1** — To limit the probability that a person in or adjacent to the building will be exposed to hazardous substances stored or used in the building.

**OH5.2** — To limit the probability that a person in or adjacent to the building will be exposed to hazardous substances released due to fire or explosion in the building.

**OH5.3** — To limit the probability that hazardous substances will contaminate the environment as a result of storage or use in the building.

---

## OA — Accessibility

*(Does not apply to Group F Div 1 or non-daily-occupied buildings — see Section 2.1.1.2.)*

**OA** — To limit the probability that, as a result of the design or construction of the building, a person with a physical or sensory disability will have unacceptable difficulty gaining access to or using the building or its facilities.

---

### OA1 — Barrier-Free Path of Travel

**OA1** — To limit the probability that a person with a physical or sensory disability will be unable to gain access to the building or move freely within it.

**OA1.1** — To limit the probability that a person with a physical or sensory disability will be unable to reach the entrance of the building or facility.

**OA1.2** — To limit the probability that a person with a physical or sensory disability will be unable to move freely within the building.

---

### OA2 — Usability

**OA2** — To limit the probability that a person with a physical or sensory disability will be unable to use the facilities of the building.

**OA2.1** — To limit the probability that a person with a physical or sensory disability will be unable to use sanitary facilities in the building.

**OA2.2** — To limit the probability that a person with a physical or sensory disability will be unable to use other facilities and services in the building.

---

## OP — Fire and Structural Protection of the Building

**OP** — To limit the probability that, as a result of the design or construction of the building, a building will be subject to an unacceptable level of damage.

---

### OP1 — Fire Protection of the Building

**OP1** — To limit the probability that, as a result of the design or construction of the building, a fire will cause an unacceptable level of damage to the building.

**OP1.1** — To limit the probability that fire will spread within a building beyond the room or suite of origin.

**OP1.2** — To limit the probability that fire will spread from one building to another.

**OP1.3** — To limit the probability that a fire will cause structural failure of the building.

**OP1.4** — To limit the probability that fire will damage essential services or systems in the building.

---

### OP2 — Structural Sufficiency of the Building

**OP2** — To limit the probability that the building will be subject to an unacceptable level of structural damage due to loads.

**OP2.1** — To limit the probability that the building will suffer structural damage as a result of loads imposed on it in normal use.

**OP2.2** — To limit the probability that the building will suffer structural damage as a result of environmental loads.

---

### OP3 — Protection of the Building from Moisture

**OP3** — To limit the probability that the building will be subject to an unacceptable level of damage due to moisture.

**OP3.1** — To limit the probability that moisture will cause deterioration of the building or its components.

---

### OP4 — Protection of Adjacent Buildings

**OP4** — To limit the probability that damage to one building will cause unacceptable damage to adjacent buildings.

**OP4.1** — To limit the probability that fire, explosion, or structural failure in one building will cause unacceptable damage to adjacent buildings.

---

## OE — Environment

*(Applies only to buildings governed by Section 9.36. of Division B — see Section 2.1.1.2.)*

**OE** — To limit the probability that, as a result of the design or construction of the building, the environment will be exposed to an unacceptable risk of harm.

---

### OE1 — Energy Efficiency

**OE1** — To limit the probability that, as a result of the design or construction of the building, energy will be consumed at levels that are unnecessarily harmful to the environment.

**OE1.1** — To limit the probability that a building will use excessive amounts of energy for heating and cooling.

**OE1.2** — To limit the probability that a building will use excessive amounts of energy for service water heating and lighting.

---

### OE2 — Greenhouse Gas Emissions

**OE2** — To limit the probability that, as a result of the design or construction of the building, greenhouse gas emissions will occur at levels that are unnecessarily harmful to the environment.

**OE2.1** — To limit the probability that a building will generate greenhouse gas emissions in excess of acceptable levels.
