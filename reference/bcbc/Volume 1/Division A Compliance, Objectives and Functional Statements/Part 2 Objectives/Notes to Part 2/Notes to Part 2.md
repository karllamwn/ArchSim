# BCBC 2024 — Division A, Part 2 Objectives
## Notes to Part 2

> Notes have **no legal effect** (per 1.1.3.1.), but provide explanatory guidance on the intent of objectives.

---

## A-2.1.1.2.(6) — Application of Objective OE (Environment)

Objective **OE** (Environment) applies only in respect of buildings or portions of buildings governed by **Section 9.36.** of Division B.

Section 9.36. covers **Energy Efficiency** requirements for buildings subject to Part 9 of Division B. These are generally small buildings, including:
- Houses and small residential buildings
- Small commercial buildings not exceeding the scope of Part 9

Large buildings governed by Part 3 or Part 4 of Division B are not covered by OE under Division A. The scope of OE is therefore limited to the Part 9 building types for which energy efficiency requirements are explicitly set out in Division B.

## A-2.2.1.1.(1) — Objective Numbering and Defined Terms

### Numbering Gaps

The objective codes in Section 2.2 contain **apparent gaps** in numbering (e.g., OH3 is followed by OH4 with no OH3.2 etc.). This is intentional — the BCBC maintains a **master numbering list** across all BC Code documents (Book I — Building Code, Book II — Fire Code, and the BC Fire Code). Numbers are reserved to ensure consistency across documents, even where specific sub-objectives are not used in a particular Book.

### "Building" in the Objectives

The term "**building**" in the objectives refers to the **building being designed, constructed, or assessed** — not to buildings in general. Each objective and functional statement is evaluated in the context of the specific building subject to the Code requirement.

### "Emergency" in OS1

The term "**emergency**" in the OS1 objectives (Fire Safety) is **not limited to fire emergencies**. It encompasses any situation requiring evacuation or emergency response involving the building. This includes:
- Fire
- Structural failure
- Hazardous substance release
- Other emergency events requiring egress

### "Person" in OS1

The term "**person**" in OS1 objectives includes **firefighters and other emergency responders**, not only building occupants. This is important because some OS1 sub-objectives relate to the ability of firefighters to conduct operations in the building (e.g., OS1.5 — impairment of firefighting operations).

This means that requirements supporting firefighter access, suppression systems, and fire compartmentation are linked to OS1 objectives even though they primarily benefit emergency responders rather than general occupants.
