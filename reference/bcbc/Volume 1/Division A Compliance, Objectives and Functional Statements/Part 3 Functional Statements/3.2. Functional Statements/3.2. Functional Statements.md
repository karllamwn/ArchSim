# BCBC 2024 — Division A, Part 3 Functional Statements
## Section 3.2 — Functional Statements

> **3.2.1.1.(1)** The functional statements of this Code are as set out in this Section.

Functional statements describe the specific functions that Division B requirements are intended to achieve. They link Division B provisions to the objectives in Part 2.

---

## Fire Safety (F01–F06)

**F01** — To limit the probability of ignition.

**F02** — To limit the probability that, if a fire starts, it will spread beyond the room or suite of origin.

**F03** — To limit the probability that, if a fire starts, smoke or toxic gases will spread beyond the room of origin.

**F04** — To limit the probability that, if a fire starts in a building, a person in or adjacent to the building will be unable to safely evacuate.

**F05** — To limit the probability that, if a fire starts in a building, the ability of firefighters to perform their function will be impaired.

**F06** — To limit the probability that, if a fire starts in a building, structural failure of the building will occur.

---

## Means of Egress (F10–F13)

**F10** — To limit the probability that a person will be unable to safely move from any occupied point in a building to a safe place outside.

**F11** — To limit the probability that a person will be delayed in safely evacuating a building in an emergency.

**F12** — To limit the probability that a person will be unable to identify a safe evacuation route from the building.

**F13** — To limit the probability that a person will be unable to safely use an exit.

---

## Structural Safety (F20–F23)

**F20** — To limit the probability that the building will experience structural failure as a result of loads imposed on it in normal use.

**F21** — To limit the probability that the building will experience structural failure as a result of environmental loads, including wind, snow, rain, and earthquake.

**F22** — To limit the probability that the building will experience structural failure due to deterioration of structural materials.

**F23** — To limit the probability that a person will be injured due to structural failure or movement of the building.

---

## Safety in Use (F30–F36)

**F30** — To limit the probability that a person will be injured due to a fall in or on the building.

**F31** — To limit the probability that a person will be injured due to the design or construction of stairs, ramps, or other means of vertical movement.

**F32** — To limit the probability that a person will be injured due to the design or construction of guards, handrails, or similar protective elements.

**F33** — To limit the probability that a person will be injured due to contact with sharp, hot, or electrically charged surfaces or elements in the building.

**F34** — To limit the probability that a person will be injured due to an explosion caused by or in the building.

**F35** — To limit the probability that a person will be injured due to contact with or exposure to mechanical equipment in the building.

**F36** — To limit the probability that a person will be injured while using parking facilities associated with the building.

---

## Contaminants and Indoor Air Quality (F40–F46)

**F40** — To limit the probability that a person in the building will be exposed to contaminants in the indoor air.

**F41** — To limit the probability that potable water in the building will be contaminated.

**F42** — To limit the probability that hazardous substances will be released within the building.

**F43** — To limit the probability that a person in the building will be exposed to harmful concentrations of combustion products.

**F44** — To limit the probability that indoor air quality will be harmful to the health of building occupants due to inadequate ventilation.

**F45** — To limit the probability that hazardous substances stored or used in the building will be released into the environment.

**F46** — To limit the probability that radon or other ground gases will accumulate in the building to unacceptable levels.

---

## Indoor Conditions (F50–F56)

**F50** — To limit the probability that thermal conditions in the building will be unacceptable for habitation.

**F51** — To limit the probability that lighting conditions in the building will be unacceptable for habitation.

**F52** — To limit the probability that condensation will occur on or within building assemblies to the extent that would be harmful to occupants or the building.

**F53** — To limit the probability that sound conditions in the building will be unacceptable for habitation.

**F54** — To limit the probability that vibration conditions in the building will be unacceptable for habitation.

**F55** — To limit the probability that a person in the building will be exposed to unacceptable electromagnetic fields or radiation.

**F56** *(Dwelling units only — see Section 3.1.1.2.)* — To limit the probability that a person in a dwelling unit will be exposed to unacceptable levels of noise from other parts of the building or from outside the building.

---

## Moisture Protection (F60–F63)

**F60** — To limit the probability that moisture will cause deterioration of the building or its components.

**F61** — To limit the probability that rain or ground water penetration will cause damage to the building or harm to occupants.

**F62** — To limit the probability that condensation within building assemblies will cause deterioration of materials.

**F63** — To limit the probability that moisture from plumbing or mechanical systems will cause damage to the building.

---

## Plumbing and Sanitation (F70–F72)

**F70** — To limit the probability that a person in or adjacent to the building will be exposed to unsanitary conditions due to inadequate plumbing.

**F71** — To limit the probability that potable water will be contaminated by the building's plumbing system.

**F72** — To limit the probability that a person in or adjacent to the building will be exposed to unsanitary conditions due to inadequate drainage.

---

## Accessibility (F73–F75)

*(F73 and F74 do not apply to houses, Group F Div 1, or non-daily-occupied buildings — see Section 3.1.1.2.)*

**F73** — To limit the probability that a person with a physical or sensory disability will be unable to gain access to the building or move freely within it.

**F74** — To limit the probability that a person with a physical or sensory disability will be unable to use the facilities and services in the building.

**F75** — To limit the probability that a person with a physical or sensory disability will be unable to safely evacuate the building in an emergency.

---

## Durability (F80–F82)

**F80** — To limit the probability that the building will deteriorate to an extent that would affect its safety or habitability within its expected service life.

**F81** — To limit the probability that materials and components will deteriorate due to exposure to weather, moisture, or other environmental conditions.

**F82** — To limit the probability that the building will suffer unacceptable damage due to inadequate maintenance provisions.

---

## Energy Efficiency (F90–F101)

*(Apply only to buildings governed by Section 9.36. of Division B)*

**F90** — To limit the probability that the building will use excessive amounts of energy for space heating and cooling.

**F91** — To limit the probability that the building will use excessive amounts of energy due to inadequate thermal performance of the building envelope.

**F92** — To limit the probability that the building will use excessive amounts of energy due to uncontrolled air leakage.

**F93** — To limit the probability that the building will use excessive amounts of energy for service water heating.

**F94** — To limit the probability that the building will use excessive amounts of energy for lighting.

**F95** — To limit the probability that the building will use excessive amounts of energy for mechanical ventilation.

**F96** — To limit the probability that the building's energy systems will perform less efficiently than designed.

**F97** — To limit the probability that the building will generate greenhouse gas emissions in excess of acceptable levels due to the choice of energy source.

**F98** — To limit the probability that the building will generate greenhouse gas emissions due to the inefficient use of energy for space conditioning.

**F99** — To limit the probability that the building will generate greenhouse gas emissions due to the inefficient use of energy for water heating.

**F100** — To limit the probability that the building will generate greenhouse gas emissions due to the inefficient use of energy for lighting and equipment.

**F101** — To limit the probability that the building will generate greenhouse gas emissions due to the choice of materials or construction methods.
